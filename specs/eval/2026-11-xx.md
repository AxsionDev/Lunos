## Where the traffic went

Every model request goes through the harness's metering proxy. The upstream hosts it forwarded to, per model, must be EU hosts only:

- `mistral/devstral-medium-latest` (eu): TBD (expected `api.eu.mistral.ai`)
- `mistral/mistral-medium-latest` (eu): TBD (expected `api.eu.mistral.ai`)

- `stackit/openai/gpt-oss-120b` (eu): TBD (expected `api.openai-compat.model-serving.eu01.onstackit.cloud`)

## What this changes in Lunos

After the results are published, copy each model's entry into `packages/opencode/src/provider/eval-results.json`. Under an EU-only residency policy, Lunos then recommends, and defaults to, the best EU model that passed at least 50% of the tasks. If none did, Recommended stays empty for EU users.
